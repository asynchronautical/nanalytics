A green banana means people have an actual choice. The rest were decided, to varying degrees, when the lines were drawn.

The rule

stage = 1 + round( 9 × min(|margin|, 20) / 20 )

margin = Harris minus Trump, 2024, counted inside the district as it is drawn for 2026. Stage 1 is green, stage 10 is black. Anything past a 20-point lean is treated as fully gone.

This is a structural measure. It says nothing about who is running, who has money, or what the national mood is. It asks one question: before any campaign, how far from a coin flip is this district? Expert handicappers answer a different question: who will win.
