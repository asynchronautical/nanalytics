Nanalytics

A ripening banana as a status glyph.

One banana, one fixed rig, about four weeks. 35 frames, 10 named stages, served by URL.

Dashboards run on numbers, and numbers convey certainty. A 2 is always a 2. But the apparent clarity of a number can be a bit of a cognitive honey trap, presenting as clear something that really should remain ambiguous, at least a bit. A ripening banana still tells you something, how fresh, how far gone, roughly how long you've got, but above all it says “think about me a bit more.” And that's the point.

Use it where a number might overstate your confidence: data freshness, how long a project has sat unloved, how far past the oil change marker your car is, anything that ages. Don't use it where someone needs the number. You should probably still report vital signs and lab results as actual numbers.

Try it

Use it

One image tag. Pass any number, tell it the range, and the service answers with the matching banana. Works anywhere an image URL works: a dashboard, a README, a status page, a spreadsheet cell.

<img src="https://nanalytics.dev/v1/banana?value=37&min=0&max=90">

Google Sheets:  =IMAGE("https://nanalytics.dev/v1/banana?value=" & A2 & "&min=0&max=90")

Add &theme=dark for dark backgrounds, &mode=frame for all 35 frames instead of 10 stages, &size=64 for a smaller file. No value, and you get a deliberately blank "unknown" banana, never a fresh one by default. The full vocabulary is in the spec.
